What is Rideshare G2?

Rideshare G2 by Squire is a plugin for Even G2 glasses that shows your current Uber ride: status, approximate driver distance, pickup ETA, driver name and license plate. Once the ride starts, it shows your drop-off ETA.

What do I need?

Even G2 glasses, an iPhone with the Even Realities app, and an Uber account. Find Rideshare G2 by Squire in the G2 Marketplace.

Can I book or cancel a ride from my glasses?

No. Rideshare G2 is read-only by design. Book, change and pay for rides in the Uber app as usual.

Does it work with other ride services?

Not yet. Rideshare G2 works with Uber only.

What happens to my ride data?

Ride details are held in memory only while your trip is active and are cleared when it ends. Read the privacy policy for the full picture.

Is Squire part of Uber or Even Realities?

No. Squire is an independent developer and is not affiliated with Uber Technologies, Inc. or Even Realities.
